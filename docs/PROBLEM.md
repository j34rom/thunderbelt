# Thunderbelt — Recycling Schedule (working title)

## Who
A student who lives in a residential area with little or no waste
management. Testers before Session 3: the team's relatives and
neighbours, using their phones.

## The struggle
Observed first-hand by the team:
- After finishing a plastic bottle, they do not know where to put it, so
  they throw it around or use one bin for everything.
- Pick-up and drop-off dates change with no fixed date, and nobody they
  know can say who runs them.
- They also mentioned long queues and a lack of incentives (both parked,
  see `docs/LATER.md`).

## Today's workaround
Throw it around whenever possible, or put everything in one bin and wait
for the garbage truck to arrive on a random day. Recycling does not
happen, and the cost is wasted waste and low participation.

## The MVP
A phone-friendly website where a resident picks their area and sees the
latest pick-up and drop-off schedule for it, entered by the team as the
admin.

## How we'll know it worked
Demo (about three minutes): the admin adds a drop-off for Saturday at
2pm in our area. On a phone, a resident opens the site, picks the area,
and sees the collection at the top with date, time and place.
Before Session 3, relatives and neighbours try it on their own phones.

## Out of scope
- Resident accounts, sign-up and saved area
- Progress tracking, points and redeemable vouchers
- The incentives program and business partners
- Barangay officials as admins
- Residents posting their own collections
- Exact home addresses, maps and GPS "nearest" search
- Mobile numbers and SMS reminders
- Queue tracking

## Glossary
- **Collection**: one scheduled pick-up or drop-off, with a type, area,
  place, date and time.
- **Pick-up**: the truck comes to you.
- **Drop-off**: you bring your recyclables to a place.
- **Area**: a barangay, purok or street name picked from a list. Never a
  home address.
- **Admin**: the team (the website's creators), who sign in by emailed
  link with no password and add collections. The only signed-in user.
- **Resident**: anyone who opens the site to read the schedule. No
  account needed.
